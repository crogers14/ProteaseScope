Proteolytic digestion was predicted in silico with ProteaseScope 0.2.0. Proteins (human_histones.fasta (54 entries)) were digested with: Histone PTM baseline: Trypsin (PSI-MS), propionylated K (4-40 aa, up to 1 missed cleavage). Cleavage rules (zero-width regular expressions): Trypsin (PSI-MS): (?<=[KR])(?!P) (PSI-MS MS:1001251). PSI-MS rules were taken from psi-ms.obo version 4.2.17. Sequential digests used the union of the cut sites of their rules. Chemistry: carbamidomethylation of cysteine (+57.021464 Da) as a fixed modification; propionylation: protein + peptide N-term; initiator methionine removal: MetAP rule. Peptides within the length window were considered identifiable. Uniqueness was assessed against UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot reviewed human proteome UP000005640 incl. isoforms, UniProt release 2026_04 (04-November-2026; downloaded 2026-11-14), 42,545 entries + human_histones.fasta (54 entries) (0 not already present); a peptide maps to every entry whose sequence contains it (I = L). All results are predictions and were not observed experimentally.
